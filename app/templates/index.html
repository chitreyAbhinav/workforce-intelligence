🎯 Problem Context & Hackathon Alignment
SAS CU Hackathon Challenge Context

Organizations face unprecedented talent friction: generic career guidance fails because it merely catalogs job listings without linking market demand to empirical employee outcomes.

Our Core Defensible Thesis:
"We don't merely tell you what jobs are available. We connect market demand across 15,800+ listings, required technical skills, observed junior hike outcomes (JDS), and senior leadership characteristics (SDS) to tell you which skills and career pathways are strategically worth pursuing — and explain exactly why."

4 Supplied Datasets Ingested:

  • Data Science Jobs: 1,602 company postings with experience ranges and LPA salaries.
  • Analytics Jobs: 12,064 cleaned postings with unstructured descriptions and skills.
  • JDS Skill Traits: 139 Junior Data Scientists evaluated across 5 skill vectors & hike outcome.
  • SDS Personality Traits: 160 Senior Data Scientists evaluated on Big Five (OCEAN) traits & success.

🏗️ System Architecture & 12 Locked Features
End-to-End Analytics Workflow Pipeline
1. Data Quality & Cleansing Engine: Profiling, Tukey IQR outlier filters, LPA & Experience parsers.
2. Job Market & Skill Demand Engines: NLP extraction over 12K listings, salary premiums by skill.
3. Predictive ML & XAI Engine: JDS Random Forest & SDS Personality classifiers with SHAP attribution.
4. AI Career GPS & Adjacency Graph: Cosine distance pathfinding, transition feasibility, and milestones.
5. Skill ROI & Objective Optimizers: Marginal hike gain * market demand * salary premium formula.
6. Digital Twin & Workforce Simulator: Cohort simulation & Build vs. Buy economic ROI modeling.
